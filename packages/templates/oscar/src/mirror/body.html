Frequently

Is Oscar Education KHDA approved?

Yes. Oscar Education is approved by KHDA, the Knowledge and Human Development Authority of Dubai, and has been training students in Dubai since 1993.

How do I enroll in a course?

Call us or visit our training center near Al Nahda Metro Station in Al Qusais, Dubai, and our team will help you choose a course and get enrolled.

What departments do you offer?

We offer four departments: Computer & Information Technology, Accounts & Finance, Design & Digital, and Languages, covering a wide range of courses.

Do you offer certificates?

Ask our team about certification options for your specific course when you enroll.

Can I visit the center before enrolling?

Yes, you're welcome to visit our training center in Al Qusais, Dubai, before enrolling. Call us to arrange a visit.
